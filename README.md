# TensorVok-POS_Retail_Android_APP: Professional AI-Powered POS & Retail Management System

**TensorVok-POS_Retail_Android_APP** (Unified Handheld Mobile Checkout System) is an all-in-one, full-stack intelligent checkout and business management ecosystem developed by **TensorVok**. Focused on high performance, industrial-grade stability, and deep hardware integration, this system provides a comprehensive solution—from cloud-based management to localized edge-device deployment—for the retail and hospitality sectors.

---

## 🚀 System Architecture & Modules

Our modular design covers every operational facet of modern business:

* **Cloud-Based SaaS Dashboard (Vue.js):** A centralized digital management hub supporting multi-merchant account management, global configuration, and cross-platform data analytics.
* **Retail Client (Native Java/Kotlin):** Optimized for high-velocity retail environments, supporting rapid barcode scanning, real-time inventory synchronization, and extensive peripheral integration.
* **Hospitality/F&B Client (Native Java/Kotlin):** Purpose-built for dining environments, featuring table management, kitchen order printing, and dynamic menu customization.
* **QR-Code Self-Ordering System:** A lightweight mobile solution enabling contactless ordering and streamlined payment workflows.
* **Merchant Management Portals (Mobile/Web):** Specialized retail and hospitality backends allowing owners to monitor real-time sales performance and staff productivity from anywhere.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

With over 12 years of software R&D experience, we have chosen the most stable and hardware-centric technology path:

* **Android Frontend:** Developed with **Native Java & Kotlin** to ensure maximum compatibility and operational efficiency with thermal printers, scanners, and payment terminals.
* **Web Backend & Dashboards:** Built on **Vue.js** to provide an ultra-responsive and modern UI/UX.
* **Core Logic:** Optimized for high-concurrency scenarios to ensure low-latency payment processing and data synchronization.

## 🤖 AI Capabilities & Enterprise Features

While we offer the core POS client as an open-source solution, we provide advanced **AI Agent** and **Edge AI Vision** modules for enterprise clients:
